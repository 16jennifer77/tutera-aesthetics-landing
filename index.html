Questions

Asked from the chair.

Which treatment is right for me?

That is what the consultation is for. Your provider looks at your face and skin as a whole, listens to what bothers you, and explains which options fit, whether that is wrinkle-relaxing injections, dermal filler, microneedling, a medical-grade peel, skin tightening, or simply a good skincare plan for now. You leave with a clear plan and no pressure to start anything.

What does it cost?

Your initial consultation is free, so you can get answers before committing to anything. Because the right plan depends on the treatment, your provider goes over any costs clearly with you, with no surprises. Call (480) 874-1515 with questions.

Will I look overdone?

A natural-looking result is the goal here. Our providers plan every treatment conservatively, with you, so you look like a refreshed version of yourself rather than different. Outcomes vary from person to person, and your provider sets honest expectations at your consultation.

Who performs the treatments?

Tutera Medical is physician-founded. Aesthetic treatments are performed by skilled, licensed providers, under physician direction, at all three offices: Scottsdale, Chandler and Glendale.

Is there downtime?

It depends on the treatment. Many people return to their day after injectables or filler, while microneedling and peels come with a short recovery your provider will describe in detail. What to expect for your specific plan, and how long results typically last, is explained before you decide anything.

Is it right for me?

Aesthetic treatments are elective and not right for everyone. What is appropriate is decided at a consultation with a licensed provider, and a free consultation is where that decision is made together. We will be honest about what will help, what won't, and when the answer is to wait or do less.
